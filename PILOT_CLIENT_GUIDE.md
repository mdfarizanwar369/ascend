# Ascend Pilot Guide: Clients

Welcome to the Ascend pilot. Ascend helps you stay consistent between training sessions without making fitness feel like homework.

## Join The Pilot

1. Open `https://www.getascend.fit` on your phone.
2. Tap **Get started** and choose **Client**.
3. Enter your real name, email, password, and the referral code from your gym or trainer.
4. Complete your goal and daily-guide details.
5. Add Ascend to your home screen for quicker access.

## Your Two-Minute Daily Routine

1. Open **Home** and read your next best action.
2. Log meals with a photo. Always review the AI estimate before saving.
3. Log water and any activity completed that day.
4. Log weight on the schedule agreed with your trainer, not necessarily every day.
5. Complete your daily mission or habits when applicable.

Your Momentum Score rewards consistency across food, weight, water, and habits. It is a guide, not a grade.

## Stay Connected

- Use **Message your trainer** for coaching questions when you have a trainer.
- Use **AI Coach** for practical nutrition guidance if your plan includes it.
- Check **Weekly report** to see patterns and progress.
- Add progress photos only when you are comfortable. They are private to your account and authorized coaching team.

## Food Photo Tips

- Photograph the whole plate in good light.
- Keep the camera steady and avoid heavy filters.
- Edit the food name, portion, calories, or macros if the estimate looks wrong.
- AI estimates are approximate and do not replace medical or dietetic advice.

## Need Help?

Open **? Support** in Ascend or email `support@getascend.fit`. Include what you tapped, what you expected, and a screenshot if possible. Never send your password.
