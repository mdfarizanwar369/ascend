# Coach Zoe workout engine V2

V2 uses the existing Gemini workout call. It reads completed exercise names from recent `burn_log` events and names from recently generated plans, chooses a session locally from reviewed movements, and gives Gemini a compact session blueprint. The server enforces that blueprint after Gemini responds. Exercise swaps and the three-session roadmap use local logic; they do not call Gemini.

The reviewed prescription catalog has 120 movements. The Full Gym options include leg curl, leg extension, two leg press formats, hack squat, shoulder press, chest fly, rear delt, supported row, hip abduction, calf raise, and cable variations. Forty-five and sixty minute muscle-gain sessions use accessory slots rather than repeating push/pull slots. The selector balances recent accessory targets, push/pull angles, and available equipment. Accessory swaps stay within the same target area. Every catalog entry has a local visual and coaching instructions; the v6 through v9 visuals resolve only for V2 workouts until its public flag is enabled.

V2 exposes more precise equipment choices in the existing fourth builder step. Home bodyweight no longer assumes a chair or low step; each has its own choice. Hotel Room uses compact, quiet movement and does not assume a chair, bench, step, or walking route. A long resistance band is named explicitly because the band exercises use that form of band. Outdoor route, park bench, low exercise bar, pull-up bar, and mat are separate choices. A route supports walking intervals, a fixed bench supports incline push-ups and sit-to-stands, and a secure low bar supports inverted rows. Outdoor plans without a mat exclude floor exercises and wall-dependent movements from both prescriptions and swaps. When equipment cannot support both upper-body patterns, Zoe adjusts the session focus rather than calling it full-body strength. Running intervals and pull-ups require completed exercise history; a plan alone never establishes ability.

Equipment selection now has a visible effect across active sessions. A chosen low step gets a knee-dominant slot, and a first-time pull-up bar gets a brief, controlled Short Bar Hang rather than an unverified pull-up. A bar hold can swap to a lighter standing upper-back movement if the bar is unsuitable; the instructions explain that this is not equivalent resistance. A secure low bar has an easier upright row option. Full pull-ups and hanging knee raises still require completed history. A walking route is used in the warm-up and cooldown even when a 20-minute strength plan has no cardio slot. Recovery sessions can set equipment aside; the existing “why today” text explains that choice. The catalog and illustrations expanded, while the Gemini call count is unchanged.

Each time choice targets a session near the selected length: roughly 17–22 minutes for 20, 26–31 for 30, 39–46 for 45, and 59–62 for 60+. V2 now estimates plan length from the prescribed sets, reps or timed holds, between-set rests, a one-minute transition between exercises, a four-minute warm-up, and a three-minute cooldown. The duration is shown as an approximate single-minute estimate, recalculates after swaps, and is also used when saving an older owner-pilot V2 plan. Short plans can trim a cardio fallback to stay close to the member's window. Zoe uses comfortable movement to fill unused time instead of adding hard sets to cautious or recovery work. Hotel-room movement stays quiet and compact: long room-cardio blocks are capped, recovery plans add distinct gentle mobility, and a plan never pairs two nearly identical marching blocks. A room-cardio swap can use a shorter mobility movement when the other cardio choices would repeat a march. Actual elapsed time will vary with pace and rest.

The builder audit also tightened the whole path: V2's limited gym choice now says “Dumbbells at Gym,” outdoor and bodyweight focus labels reflect missing resistance patterns, and Gemini receives the calculated session length. Empty but genuine workout logs and synced same-day sessions can trigger recovery. Swaps exclude movements already in the plan, and the UI hides a swap when none remains valid. Local refresh avoids choosing the same alternative twice. Verified exercise-detail logging now follows the same V2 rollout flag as plan generation; the public flag remains off during the owner pilot.

Easy finishing movement is included in the prescription. Zoe shows a provisional duration estimate, and swaps recalculate it. A lighter or shorter swap can shorten the session; Zoe does not inflate a room-sized movement to match a long walk. If an avoid list leaves too few suitable movements, the plan explains why it is shorter. A member can enter actual minutes on save; estimates are still used when they leave that field blank. The pull-up bar starts with a short hold and increases its target only after a member logs controlled seconds without rating the session too hard. Pull-ups and hanging knee raises remain gated on completed history. Every fresh V2 plan exercise in the tested goal, duration, setting, and conservative matrix has at least one reviewed swap. An older saved iPhone V2 plan gains missing safe swaps when reopened, without regenerating or charging another Gemini call. A member can save after completing any one exercise; the existing workout log stores only the checked exercises, and reopening an iPhone daily workout retains that partial completion state.

## Rollout

- `COACH_ZOE_WORKOUT_ENGINE_V2=false` and `COACH_ZOE_WORKOUT_ENGINE_V2_OWNER_PILOT=false` are the defaults. Existing users keep the V1 response and planner behavior.
- Set only `COACH_ZOE_WORKOUT_ENGINE_V2_OWNER_PILOT=true` for an owner-only pilot. The server must use `AI_PROVIDER=gemini`. The fourth-step equipment choices use the same server-calculated access flag, so public users continue to see the existing builder choices while V2 is owner-only.
- Review exercise suitability, completed workout history, iOS daily saves and swaps, and Gemini usage per workout in the pilot before enabling `COACH_ZOE_WORKOUT_ENGINE_V2=true` for everyone.
- Public rollout is a separate flag change after real owner sessions have been checked for suitability, timing, partial saves, and costs. A green automated suite does not replace that live pilot.
- Set both flags back to `false` to roll back. Saved V2 workouts still display because their extra response fields are optional and versioned.

## Cost and data

Generation still makes one Gemini request. Swaps and refreshes use local alternatives. V2 sends a compact history and blueprint instead of the legacy broad context; it does not add a model, provider, database table, or background job. Exact provider billing may vary with request and response tokens, so usage should be compared during the owner pilot.

Completed workouts record exercise names and optional effort (`too_easy`, `about_right`, `too_hard`) in the existing event metadata. A checked Zoe plan is treated as a completed plan, not proof of performed sets or lifted weight. Web plans save prescribed exercise names; owner-pilot V2 plans also retain an account-bound copy for optional performance logging; iOS daily plans are read from their existing saved workout rows. Planned exercises influence variety, but never count as completed training or trigger recovery. The next plan uses recent completed-workout effort, confirmed logged performance, and exercise history while rotating away from repeated movements. The roadmap is a preview that adapts after later workouts.

V2 saves completed exercise indexes, planned and completed counts, actual or estimated session minutes, and the completed names in the same workout event. Unchecked exercises are excluded from future completed history. Retry with the same workout key reuses the existing burn log. After enough recovery, Zoe can reuse one or two familiar main movements to make progress easier to recognize while varying the remaining session. If the most recent session felt too hard, the target stays conservative.

The owner pilot can optionally record actual sets, reps, weight, or minutes from a generated plan. This uses the same workout completion key and `burn_log`, so one session contributes one calorie and momentum entry. The server verifies the saved iOS daily plan or the owner account's generated V2 plan before accepting observed values. Recorded exercises are marked as observed performance; the full completed plan remains available for recovery and variety decisions. The next V2 plan can show a conservative target based on confirmed prior performance. The standalone Detailed Workout and Quick Activity screens remain available for sessions without a Zoe plan. This flow adds no Gemini request. It is visible only to owner accounts while V2 is available to them.
