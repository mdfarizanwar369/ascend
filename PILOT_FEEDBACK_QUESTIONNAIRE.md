# Ascend Pilot Feedback Questionnaire

Use this after the first week and again at the end of the pilot. Score questions from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

## Clients

1. I understood what to do when I opened Ascend.
2. Logging my day felt quick and manageable.
3. Ascend helped me stay aware of my goal between sessions.
4. The Momentum Score felt encouraging and understandable.
5. Food photo estimates were useful after I reviewed them.
6. Trainer or AI support felt relevant rather than intrusive.
7. I would keep using Ascend after the pilot.
8. What was the most useful moment this week?
9. Where did you feel confused or frustrated?
10. What is the one thing you would improve?

## Trainers

1. I could quickly identify which clients needed attention.
2. Ascend reduced guessing between training sessions.
3. Client information was clear enough to guide a check-in.
4. Praise and Daily Missions were fast to use.
5. Ascend did not feel like extra admin work.
6. I would use Ascend with paying clients.
7. Which signal led to a useful client conversation?
8. Which alert or screen felt unnecessary?
9. What would make Ascend indispensable to your coaching?

## Gym Owners

1. Trainer approvals and client assignments were manageable.
2. I could understand engagement across the gym.
3. Referral attribution was useful.
4. Ascend could improve member retention or PT value.
5. The operating effort felt reasonable.
6. I would expand the pilot to more members.
7. What business outcome matters most for the next 30 days?
8. What blocked adoption?
9. What evidence would justify paying for Ascend?
