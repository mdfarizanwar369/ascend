# Ascend Pilot Guide: Trainers

Ascend should save attention, not create admin. Your job is to notice the right client at the right time.

## Join And Get Approved

1. Open `https://www.getascend.fit` and create a **Trainer** account.
2. Use your gym referral code.
3. Ask the owner to approve your trainer role and assign your clients.
4. Confirm that **Trainer** appears in the bottom navigation.

## Five-Minute Daily Routine

1. Open **Trainer**.
2. Review **Clients needing attention today**.
3. Contact only the clients with a clear reason to act.
4. Use **Send praise** when a client is building momentum.
5. Assign one simple Daily Mission only when it helps the client’s current plan.

If the dashboard says everyone is steady, that is good news. You do not need to manufacture extra work.

## Client Review

Open a client to review:

- Momentum and recent activity
- Daily calories, protein, carbs, fat, and water against their guide
- Food logs and photos
- Weight trend and progress photos
- Habits, missions, messages, and weekly report

Momentum is an early signal. Use it with coaching judgment; it is not a diagnosis or a punishment score.

## Simple Coaching Standard

- Praise specific progress.
- Ask one clear question when something changes.
- Avoid commenting on every meal.
- Escalate health or nutrition concerns to a qualified professional.
- Keep sensitive client information inside Ascend.

## Need Help?

Use the support icon in Ascend or email `support@getascend.fit`. Include the client name only when necessary and never share passwords.
